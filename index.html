About Bing Building

Bing Building has manufactured its own cement bricks in Radium for about 17 years, supplying builders, wholesalers and the public. Alongside our own brick range, we stock sand, stone, cement, tools and hardware from trusted brands. Delivery and credit account options are available for trade and retail customers.

“Building your dreams” — because we believe dreams can be realities. Our products and services are our pride.
